Photohydration of an RNA Base Derivative

  • 1,3-dimethyluracil (DMU): high photohydration yield, both photoproducts absorb only in the deep UV
  • Open question: does photohydration happen on the excited $^1\pi\pi^*$ state, or the hot ground state via a twisted intermediate?
  • DMU has been used as an actinometer since the 1970s (Numao, Rahn & Sellin) — photohydration yield ~2 orders of magnitude higher than canonical RNA/DNA bases, making it a clean system to isolate the mechanism.
  • The twisted intermediate was proposed computationally by Park et al. (JPCL 2022) — a half-chair puckered ring geometry, reached via conical intersection.
  • Two very recent 2025 JACS papers (Obara et al., Mathew et al.) independently invoked this same twisted intermediate to explain long-lived (tens of ps) IR/UV transients in uracil and thymine, previously misassigned to the triplet or $^1n\pi^*$ state.
  • Resolving this needs a probe that simultaneously tracks excited-state decay (<4 eV), hot ground-state cooling, and photoproduct absorption (>5 eV) — no prior deep-UV probe had this bandwidth.
